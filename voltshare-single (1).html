How it works

  1. Search for a host near you that matches your connector.
  2. Request a time slot and wait for the host to confirm.
  3. Visit the host after confirmation and charge safely.
  4. Pay the host. The platform keeps a 20% commission (host keeps 80%).

Safety first

  • Check that the connector and charging power match your vehicle before you plug in.
  • Confirm the host has permission to share the charger and that the wiring is in good condition.
  • Never use damaged cables or charge in rain without a covered, grounded outlet.
  • If you see smoke, a burning smell or sparks, unplug only if safe, move away and call your local emergency number (112 in India).
